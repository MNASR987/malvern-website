Malvern International School

Why Malvern

We believe knowledge and values go hand in hand. We strive to develop students who are capable, honest, and principled.

International learning

An American curriculum taught through Savvas and Obelearn programmes, in classes of no more than 25 students.

Saudi identity at the heart

Arabic, Holy Quran and Islamic Studies, and Social Studies are core to every learner's week.

Japanese mathematics & programming

In partnership with SPRIX Inc. (Japan) for Grades 1–9, with the TOFAS external assessment.

Committed to quality

A member of Cognia, working through the Cognia accreditation process, and one of the schools of El-Shrouk Pioneer Academy.

Our Vision

“Empowering students through knowledge, values, innovation, and technology while guiding minds and shaping character.”
