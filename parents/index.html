Every day

School Uniform

School uniform is compulsory for all students, every school day. It helps our students feel part of one community, and it keeps the focus on learning.

Girls wear a white polo shirt with the Malvern crest. Boys wear a navy polo shirt with the Malvern crest.

The uniform is available from the school. Full details of the complete uniform and the PE kit are given when you collect it. Please label every item with your child's name.

Working together

Parent–teacher conferences

Two conferences each year — in October and April — to talk with teachers about your child's progress.

Quizzes and exams

Two quiz cycles in each semester, then a revision week and semester exams. Dates are in the calendar above.

Speak to us early

Our door is open. If something is worrying you or your child, contact the class teacher or the school office — we would always rather hear a concern early than late.
