Step by step

The Admissions Process

Six simple steps from your first question to your child's first day.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Enquire

    Send us an enquiry online, by WhatsApp or by phone.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Visit

    Book a tour of the school and meet our admissions team.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Apply

    Complete the application and submit the required documents.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Assessment

    Your child meets our team for a friendly placement assessment, so we can understand their level and support them from day one.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Offer

    We contact you with the admission decision and the next steps.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Registration

    Confirm the place by completing registration and paying the fees. We will then welcome your child and share everything you need for the first day.

Required Documents

Please bring copies of the following. Our admissions team will tell you if anything else is needed for your child's grade.

  • Student's birth certificate
  • Student's passport and Iqama, or National ID (for Saudi students)
  • Parent's or guardian's ID or Iqama
  • Latest school report and transfer certificate (from Grade 1 upwards)
  • Vaccination record
  • Recent passport-size photographs

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Which grades do you offer?

Girls from KG1 to Grade 12, and boys from KG1 to Grade 6.

Are the fees inclusive of VAT?

No. The tuition fees shown are annual fees in Saudi riyals, excluding VAT.

Which curriculum do you follow?

An American curriculum, taught through Savvas and Obelearn programmes, alongside Arabic, Holy Quran and Islamic Studies, and Social Studies.

How big are the classes?

No more than 25 students in a class.

Do you teach Arabic and Islamic Studies?

Yes. Arabic, Holy Quran and Islamic Studies, and Social Studies are taught as identity subjects.

What is the SPRIX programme?

Malvern partners with SPRIX Inc. (Japan) for mathematics and programming in Grades 1 to 9, including the TOFAS external assessment.

Is there a school uniform?

Yes. Uniform is compulsory for all students and is available from the school.

What are the school hours?

Sunday to Thursday, 6:30 am to 1:30 pm. Timings are adjusted during Ramadan.

Is the school accredited?

Malvern International School is a member of Cognia and is working through the Cognia accreditation process.

Who runs the school?

Malvern International School is one of the schools of El-Shrouk Pioneer Academy.

Do you provide school transport?

Please contact our admissions team to ask about school transport for your area.

When can my child start?

The 2026–2027 school year began on 23 August 2026. Contact our admissions team to check availability in your child's grade — we will be happy to help with joining during the year where places allow.
