## 2. The personal data we collect

We collect only the data we need. Through this website we collect:

- **Enquiry and tour requests:** parent or guardian name, mobile number, email address, the grade and section you are enquiring about, your preferred contact method and any message you choose to write.
- **Job applications:** information you send us by email, such as your CV.

We do **not** ask for children's names, identity numbers, health information or other sensitive data through this website. Please do not include such information in your message.

## 3. Why we use your data and our legal basis

We use your data only to:

- respond to your enquiry and arrange a school tour;
- manage the admissions process if you choose to apply;
- consider job applications.

We process your data on the basis of your **consent**, which you give by ticking the consent box on our forms, and where necessary to take steps at your request before an admission or employment contract.

## 4. Withdrawing consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us (see section 1). Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew.

## 9. Your rights

Under the PDPL you have the right to:

- **be informed** about how your personal data is collected and used;
- **access** your personal data held by us;
- **obtain a copy** of your personal data in a readable format;
- **request correction** of data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
- **request destruction** of data that is no longer needed;
- **withdraw your consent** at any time.

To use any of these rights, contact us (see section 1). We will respond within the period set by the law.

## 10. Complaints

If you have a concern, please contact us first. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA), the competent authority under the PDPL.
